Our Story

We met on a June morning, beneath roses that had climbed further than anyone intended — a borrowed umbrella, a sudden shower, and one long conversation neither of us wanted to end.

the first june

Seven years, two cities and one rather opinionated cat later, we are finally planting the garden we always talked about. Nothing would mean more than to have you standing beside us when we do.

always, us
