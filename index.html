№ 02 · Their Story

Our Story

I

We met on a frost-bright December morning, over the last two mince pies at a crowded market stall — a borrowed scarf, a shared laugh, and one long conversation neither of us wanted to end.

II

Six years, three homes and one rather opinionated spaniel later, we are finally opening the doors we always talked about. Nothing would mean more than to have you standing beside us when we say forever.

“Somewhere between the first hello and every promise since, we found our always.”

— a page from our notebook

written by candlelight, one december — a note we keep returning to

and forever begins
