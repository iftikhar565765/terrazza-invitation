№ 02 · Their Story

Our Story

Every love story needs a meeting point, and ours was a lemon grove above the sea — a borrowed book, a sudden summer storm, and one long conversation while the rain drew shapes on the terrace stones.

Six years, three cities and one slightly spoiled cat later, we are finally building the terrace we always talked about. Nothing would mean more than to have you standing beside us when we do.
